A dash means there is no town-supply gap on the dry path, so a share is not meaningful. Farming storages are shown beside the town gap and are not something a city’s desalination plant can fill.

What this meansThe largest town-supply gap is in Western Australia: 98 billion litres short of the 40% line by the end of March if those storages repeat their driest recent summer. A what-if 15% cut in household use from now to the end of March covers about 16% of that gap. Running desalination at last year’s pace covers about 54% of it.
