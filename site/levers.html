South Dandalup is the town supply already under the 40% line

South Dandalup (town supply, Western Australia) is at 9.1%. 8 tracked supplies are already under 40% full. Lake Menindee has read empty since 10 January 2026. Perth’s system is 38.5% full, 7.6 points down on last year.

Exact values: 1 GL is a billion litres. Shares are how much of the town-supply gap that what-if would cover, capped at 100%. The household cut is 15% of last year’s household use, scaled to the days left until 31 March 2027. Desalination and recycled water are last year’s annual supply, scaled to the same days. A dash means there is no town-supply gap on the dry path, so a share is not meaningful. Farming storages are shown beside the town gap and are not something a city’s desalination plant can fill.

What this meansThe largest town-supply gap is in Western Australia: 98 billion litres short of the 40% line by the end of March if those storages repeat their driest recent summer. A what-if 15% cut in household use from now to the end of March covers about 16% of that gap. Running desalination at last year’s pace covers about 54% of it.
